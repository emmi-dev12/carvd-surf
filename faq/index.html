faq

questions.

the facts of a custom order. still unsure? use the start form.

the board

what makes it custom?every board is made for its order. no stock boards.
what shapes?shortboard, fish, midlength and longboard. any length that suits the shape.
can i choose the materials?no. we pick the construction for your shape and your waves, so the board rides the way it should.
not sure about the length?tell us your height, weight, level and the waves you surf. we suggest one in our reply.

design

filespng, jpg, svg, pdf, ai. vector preferred. raster at 300 dpi.
no design?describe it. we help, and confirm it in the reply.
colourwe colour-match. screen and print can differ, so we send a proof.

order

howa brief, or the start form. we reply within 48 hours with a quote and a timeline.
build timewe confirm the timeline with the quote. it depends on shape, quantity and season.
deposit50% to start. the balance before the board ships.
changesfine before production. shape and length lock once the blank is cut.
