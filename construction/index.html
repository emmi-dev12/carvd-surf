construction

you pick the wave. we pick the build.

a custom board is not a sticker on a blank. we choose foam, resin and glass for the shape and the ride.

we pick the buildyou choose shape and length. we choose the construction that suits the board, the waves and how you surf.
pu / polyesterthe classic: a pu blank with a wooden stringer, glassed in polyester. the flex and feel most surfers know.
eps / epoxyan expanded polystyrene core glassed in epoxy resin. lighter and more buoyant, with a lively feel. paddles easily, and the shell is usually tougher against dings.
stringersa wooden stringer or a bamboo deck inlay adds strength and steers the flex. we pick what suits the board.
reinforcementextra reinforcement only where a board takes load and the ride needs it. we confirm it in the quote.
glassingthe glass layers on deck and bottom set how strong and how heavy a board is. lighter for performance. for schools and rentals we can go soft: an eva deck and rails over an eps core.
finsfin boxes set up for the shape: thruster, quad, twin or single, usually futures. soft or hard fins. we confirm it in the quote.
graphicsyour artwork on the deck and bottom, under the glass or sprayed on. we send a proof before anything is made.
