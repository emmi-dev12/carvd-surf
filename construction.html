construction

you pick the wave. we pick the build.

a custom board is not a sticker on a blank. we choose foam, resin and glass for the shape and the ride.

we pick the buildyou choose shape and length. we choose the construction that suits the board, the waves and how you surf.
pu / polyesterthe classic: a polyurethane foam blank with a wooden stringer, glassed in polyester resin. a familiar flex and feel that many surfers know. heavier and quicker to ding than epoxy.
eps / epoxyan expanded polystyrene core glassed in epoxy resin. lighter and more buoyant, with a lively feel. paddles easily, and the shell is usually tougher against dings.
stringersa wooden stringer down the middle adds stiffness and controls flex. some eps boards use other layouts, like carbon rails along the edges.
carboncarbon strips, patches or tape add stiffness and strength where a board takes load. used where the ride asks for it, not as decoration.
glassingthe layers of fibreglass on deck and bottom set how strong and how heavy a board is. heavier glass for fleets and rentals, lighter for performance.
finsfin boxes for common systems, set up for the shape: thruster, quad, twin or single.
graphicsyour artwork on the deck and bottom, under the glass or sprayed on. we send a proof before anything is made.
